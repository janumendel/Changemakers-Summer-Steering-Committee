Notes from Session 1

On September 10 this group spent an hour on the curriculum. Three clear things came out of it.

An introduction to how Federation actually works does belong inside the six weeks, rather than only after them. Likely most impactful when framed as the case for why communal infrastructure is built this way, and not as an "in the weeds" walkthrough of mechanics.

Fellows arrive from very different starting points, so the same six themes may need to run at two depths rather than splitting into two curricula. That is being scoped for the winter cohort.

The Fellowship says more plainly what leadership can look like. Almost nobody finishes and walks onto a board, and the program should not imply otherwise. A Fellow should finish the experience able to name the ones that fit them, and it's okay if the next step isn't directly at Federation.

The program team is working to incorporate these elements into the next version of the Fellowship — Thank you!

Next, we'll continue the conversation about what happens when the Fellowship ends

Partnerships

Three standing partnerships that give an alum somewhere to go that is neither the Fellowship nor a cold introduction to their local Federation.

  • 1
    PartnershipChangemakers × Cabinet Leadership Exchange
    Mentor pods pairing Changemakers alumni with National Young Leadership Cabinet mentors, building a pathway from Changemakers into Cabinet leadership. Includes a New York cohort with UJA-Federation of New York.
  • 2
    PartnershipRepair the World × Changemakers Ambassadors
    A service partnership placing Changemakers alumni into Repair the World's service and justice programming as trained Ambassadors.
  • 3
    PartnershipRoot & Rise
    Local, Federation-based follow-up experience for alumni after the Fellowship ends. Piloted in a small set of communities.

Experiences

Two in-person moments where alumni act as leaders rather than as participants.

  • 1
    ExperienceAdvocacy Day and Fly-Ins
    Alumni join Federation advocacy days in Washington, DC, engaging directly with elected officials on issues affecting the Jewish community.
  • 2
    ExperienceGA Changemakers Delegation
    A dedicated alumni delegation at JFNA's General Assembly, November 2026 in Washington, DC.

Continued Learning

Two ways an alum keeps building after the Fellowship, both self-paced and open to the whole Network.

  • 1
    LearningPathways
    Self-directed tracks in Community Building, Advocacy, and Giving that take an alum from interest to something they have actually built. Each track pairs learning sessions with an Innovator Leadership Lab, and alumni move at their own pace toward a certificate.
  • 2
    LearningFacilitator's Training
    Four virtual sessions that leave an alum able to run a meeting, tell their own story, and hold a room that does not already agree with them. Titled Impactful Communication for Leadership.
